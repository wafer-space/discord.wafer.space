Junction capacitance should probably be measurable via forward current against the junction and a separate replica of it's geometry but connected to a rather dedicated junction small-signal capacitance vs. bias voltage testing circuit,

and gate capacitance is even easier because the actually-manufactured geometry is pretty restricted at the end and the gate itself is a very high impedance node and I'd expect essentially all aspects of transient standard cell behavior that are relevant to be handled with extraction adjacent geometry interference fudge factors to the extracted SPICE instance parameters which you that extend should be able to prove themselves under DC conditions.

After all, on gf180mcuD we don't get any active devices that are particularly close to the litho limits, so device geometry distortion isn't going to be that strong. The one notable part, STI-induced strain, is not limited to SCL connectivity and thus calibrating extraction models of relevant geometry effects shouldn't be _too_ hard. Low key feels like it'd be an exercise in managing `dummyCOMP`/dummy-fill-blocks to cover the entire relevant spectrum of STI induced strain intensity and in a way that'll e-test nicely...
