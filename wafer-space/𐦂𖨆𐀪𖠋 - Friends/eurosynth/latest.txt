# Welcome to #🎶-eurosynth
**EuroSynth** is @Andrew Wingate's synthesizer on a chip: a complete eurorack synth voice, built entirely in digital logic and made on wafer.space Run 2 as [EURO](<https://wafer.space/ws-run2/projects/EURO.html>). Andrew looks after this channel.

You can hear it before you hold one. The [live playground](<https://anfroholic.github.io/eurosynth/web/>) runs the chip's exact reference models in your browser, so what you hear is what the silicon produces.

To dig in, start with the [showcase](<https://github.com/anfroholic/eurosynth/blob/main/docs/SHOWCASE.md>), then read the [hardware guide](<https://github.com/anfroholic/eurosynth/blob/main/docs/HARDWARE_GUIDE.md>) when you're ready to build a board. The source is at [anfroholic/eurosynth](<https://github.com/anfroholic/eurosynth>).

Got a chip? Share your boards, patches, recordings and questions here.
