[2026-11-09 2:19 p.m.] TTBot#6027
🔍 smunaut reported @urish's [Oscillating Bones](https://www.tinytapeout.com/chips/ttgf0p3/tt_um_oscillating_bones) as partially working: It oscillates at 127 MHz however it's not visible on the digital pins at all. The load from the analog external connection on the output inverter is too great, reducing the amplitude greatly and it's not enough for the digital logic that follows. You can see a tiny ( ~ 100 mV ) signal on the analog pin . If you inject an external bias through that pin to put it in the "right" zone, you can get some life out of the digital divider but barely and inconsistent.
