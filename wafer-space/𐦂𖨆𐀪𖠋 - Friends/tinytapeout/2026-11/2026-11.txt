🔍 smunaut reported @urish's [Oscillating Bones](https://www.tinytapeout.com/chips/ttgf0p3/tt_um_oscillating_bones) as partially working: It oscillates at 127 MHz however it's not visible on the digital pins at all. The load from the analog external connection on the output inverter is too great, reducing the amplitude greatly and it's not enough for the digital logic that follows. You can see a tiny ( ~ 100 mV ) signal on the analog pin . If you inject an external bias through that pin to put it in the "right" zone, you can get some life out of the digital divider but barely and inconsistent.

# Welcome to #🧩-tinytapeout
This channel is for **Tiny Tapeout** on GF180MCU: the quicker, easier and cheaper way to get your own design made on a real chip. @tnt, @Matt Venn and @urish look after this channel. TTBot posts new submissions here.

**[Tiny Tapeout on wafer.space](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts>)**: every chip, every project on it, and what is coming next.

**Chips made on wafer.space**
- Run 1: [TTP2](<https://wafer.space/ws-run1/projects/TTP2.html>), GF 0.2 standard ([52 projects](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts/ttgf0p2/ws-run1-ttp2/>))
- Run 1: [TTPG](<https://wafer.space/ws-run1/projects/TTPG.html>), GF 0.2 power-gated ([52 projects](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts/ttgf0p2/ws-run1-ttpg/>))
- Run 2: [T0P3](<https://wafer.space/ws-run2/projects/T0P3.html>), GF 0.3 ([32 projects](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts/ttgf0p3/ws-run2-t0p3/>))

- Run 2: [T26B](<https://wafer.space/ws-run2/projects/T26B.html>), GF 26b ([90 projects](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts/ttgf26b/ws-run2-t26b/>))

**Next chips**: [GF 26c](<https://app.tinytapeout.com/shuttles/ttgf26c>) and [GF 26d](<https://app.tinytapeout.com/shuttles/ttgf26d>), to be made on wafer.space Run 3. Projects close 7 December 2026.
