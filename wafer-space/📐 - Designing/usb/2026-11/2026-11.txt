What’s special about a DSS transistor?


[2026-11-25 2:02 a.m.] namibj
Long term stable "threshold voltage" even after years of exposure to drain-source voltages near the rated blocking voltage _but while the gate is very strongly on, i.e., strong inversion._


[2026-11-25 2:04 a.m.] namibj
(the surface resistivity at the drain funnels the carriers deeper into the well, further away from the oxide, thus substantially reducing hot carrier injection effects.)

import gdstk

top.name = 'gf180mcu_tnt_io__bi_24t_usb'

nl = gdstk.Library()
nl.add(top)
nl.write_gds('gf180mcu_tnt_io__bi_24t_usb.gds')
