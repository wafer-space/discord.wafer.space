[2026-09-21 7:02 a.m.] maxclerkwell
Not generated from the PCB files, but generated FOR the PCB. 
The Die is the source of truth for it's own size and pad placement. From there you can generate "rule based" landingpatterns for the PCB Layout, which also gives you the symbol. The loop-length/shape can be generated from the landingpattern, the die height and pad-placement. Additional parameters for the loops can be generated by inspecting the kicad-netlist + the dies pad-definitions + rules. With a completely filled .cob.json, you can automatically generate bonding program and electrical tests. These give you the source of truth for all further simulation.
