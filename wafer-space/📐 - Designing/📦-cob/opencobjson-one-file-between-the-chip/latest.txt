It's heat activated within 10 seconds; but AFAIK it's tacky enough near-instantly.

Like, with that knowledge, I'm confident there's sticky tape or even just using some process that attaches a tacky sheet to the back of the die that can then be placed and pressed briefly before applying hardening.
