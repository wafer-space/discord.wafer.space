[2026-10-15 7:26 a.m.] brookieoz
It's important for a couple reasons:

1 - startup native vt device creates a current that shuts itself off once the bandgap starts up, causes the right side VBE to go up first. That creates a voltage delta in the amplifier

2 - the amplifier needs current otherwise it doesn't do anything, tail resistor with negative VTH means I don't need some kind of NMOS turnaround mirror. And the current is pretty stable because it's roughly always 0.5-0.6V

3 - amplifier input common mode voltage is quite low and to keep things simple I want NMOS input with PMOS mirror with PMOS CS output to reduce systematic mismatch from different Vds on input pair

4 - native VT devices typically match a lot better than full VT devices, important when it directly impacts bandgap voltage
