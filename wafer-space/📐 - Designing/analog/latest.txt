[2026-09-04 5:42 p.m.] namibj
Why shouldn't you be able to increase the length of them?


[2026-09-04 5:53 p.m.] namibj
I don't think you want to go quite that long for your LDO; this has considerable minimum drop out at "full current" and not itself for thermal reasons, or viewed from the other direction:
long channels for your main power pass FET beyond what's needed to be safe and reliable under years of sustained current would push the minimum pass resistance so high that you'd likely hit thermal limits for drop out voltage with the pass transistor gate fully-on, instead of needing to operate under gate-not-fully-on "active regulation" conditions to drop enough voltage at full sustained current for that `I × V = I² × R = Power` to hit TDP.

You've got closed loop feedback control going on, the process variations of concern shouldn't matter much here, as long as all those fingers see the same usual voltage drop across them.
