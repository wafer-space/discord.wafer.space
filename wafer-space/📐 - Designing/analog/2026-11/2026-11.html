Like, there would be high-mux-resistance sense wires with lower leakage and probably lower-mux-resistance drive wires with higher leakage available, about half/half split I'd guess so far, that would be muxed to the DUT to normally provide multi-terminal DC bias operating point e-tests of small relevant cells using SPICE runs to automatically match/line-up bias drive conditions that are interesting/worthwhile to cover for the stack of dies. Edit: you'd perhaps measure DC current draw proportional to clock frequency of something that's electrically approximately a small inverter with the capacitance-test-subject as it's load capacitance, and a second such inverter laid out according to matched pair analog design rules that could be measured with no load capacitance for reference. I think personally I'd want to make that inverter be actually separate push/pull transistors fed from something that cooks up a dead time for the transition such that there's no shoot-through of the "technically a half-bridge" that's wiggling the DUT load capacitance. Like, a couple delay buffers delaying the edge and then a NAND between delayed and original clock to the PMOS and a NOR between delayed and original clock to the NMOS. (edited)
