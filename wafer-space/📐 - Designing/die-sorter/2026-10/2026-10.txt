[2026-10-03 4:06 a.m.] anfroholic
Hey all, I was running the machine and was asked to post a little vid. I'll post more in the near future. I have a few I want to finish quickly, then do some small upgrades (better lighting and some small things)
Currently picking some @BreakingTaps dies. 
Basic setup is 
- robot arm
- cartesian bot below wafer that pushes die off tape
- other misc boards that power solenoids, or lights, or motors and a game controller
All the boards are networked over canbus

HUD is cpython bridged onto bus as well. All control is handled through a web interface (Starlette)
- up camera finds die on nozzle
- feeder_camera locates the pocket -- place happens between offsets from these
- down camera is on arm, does mostly calibration and location of the wafer (the dicing house sticks them on there all willy nilly, it's bad)

Like I said, more coming soon, feel free to ask questions
