[2026-09-27 2:52 a.m.] anfroholic
I will have a bunch to share in the coming days, for now I'll just share a snippet of code that basically runs the whole show. 

I thought this bit was kinda beautiful. 
```py
def sort(self, todo):
    """Do the sort
    Machine consists of 3 major components. pusher, tobor, feeders
    Certain components can be working on the next die while waiting on others 
    run_together() allows for this by blocking until both are completed
    """
    for placed, die, upcoming in pipeline(todo, size=3):
        yield from self.run_together(
            self.pick(die),                   # tobor: down onto die N, pins up, lift
            self.feeder_ready(placed, die))   # feeder: past N-1's pocket, right tape, pocket found
        yield from self.run_together(
            self.carry(die),                  # tobor: up camera, align, into the pocket
            self.pusher_under(upcoming))      # pusher: already off under die N+1
        yield from self.checkpoint(die)
```
