IHPs SG13G2 magic Pcells include this structure which doesn't actually come with any parameters to adjust and doesn't appear to have any notable further documentation I could realistically find; it supposedly has some care to it for LVS&DRC to go through in some sort of acceptable manner... but that's it.

As far as I understand it's a symmetric floating latching structure, some kind of DIAC I suppose; it's for ESD protection I think.
The bottom is symmetric.
One side is metal via'd up to one higher metal terminal; same for the other.

It's all encased in DNWELL (they call it nBuLay as it's also relevant for the SiGe HBT's the G2 process offers). It's also all thick oxide.
I disabled visibility of those layers for I hope obvious reasons.
