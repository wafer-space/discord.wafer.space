[2026-10-12 12:27 p.m.] tholin
To share my personal experience: I taped out a RISC-V core on GFMPW-1, about as performant as a non-pipelined core could get, and wired it up to a SPI <-> 10/100mbps ethernet bridge (enc28j60) and the system kept dropping packets because the RISC-V CPU struggled to process them faster than they were coming in.


[2026-10-12 12:29 p.m.] tholin

[2026-10-12 12:33 p.m.] tholin
