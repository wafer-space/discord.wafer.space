Small update about the eFuse. I've tested two more chips with eFuse from the Run 1. All Wishbone and async eFuse blocks at 5V worked flawlesly. WB blocks also passed 5 million reads endurance test without a single failed bit. This makes 3 successfuly tested eFuse chips in total. Not much, but I assume this means that WB & async eFuse blocks are safe to use in 5V designs 🙂.  Now for the downside, current SPI efuse wrapper contains an error which might lead to hold violations and SPI interface not functioning properly in some cases. So **please do not use the current SPI eFuse IP**. I'm planing to release a revised SPI wrapper in a couple of weeks, together with the 3.3V compatible eFuse IP with a separate 5V supply for writing.
