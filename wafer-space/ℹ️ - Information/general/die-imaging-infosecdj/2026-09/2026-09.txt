If you're interested in looking at one, I'll probably sponsor the expenses incurred (probably don't have the budget to cover your time though).

The RX and the TX die of each 100G section are right next to another sharing the encapsulation and the fiber termination block.
On the QSFP28-DD there are just two of these units with one's bare (except for the colored cladding) fibers routed over top of the other from the strain relief cable termination.
