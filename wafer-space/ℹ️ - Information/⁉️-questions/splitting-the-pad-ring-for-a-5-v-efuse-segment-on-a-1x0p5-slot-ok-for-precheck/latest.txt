[2026-09-26 7:26 p.m.] sriharshapy
Hi all. I'm designing a half-height (1x0p5) chip for Run 3 with the 3.3 V core (as_sc_mcu7t3v3 + ocd_io). It uses two eFuse macros from Egor's compiler. Egor's Run 1 report says writes are only reliable at 5 V (~15 mA/bit), while reads work at 3.3 V. So I'd like to give the eFuses their own supply segment:
- Two `gf180mcu_ocd_io__brk5` cells on the west side isolate the last six pads: analog 3..0, DVSS 6 and DVDD 6.
- DVDD 6 goes to the eFuse voltage (<= 5.0 V while programming), and analog 0 carries VDD_EFUSE into the core.
- No template power/ground pad is removed or moved. Only DVDD 6's voltage and the two break cells change. VSS stays continuous, since brk5 only carries VSS.

Questions:
1. Will precheck accept a pad ring modified this way (break cells, one DVDD pad at a different voltage)? Is there any rule on a minimum number of DVDD/DVSS pads per segment?
2. On the Run 1 1x0p5 CoB board, DVDD 6 (bond pad 61) is on its own net `PWR_AUX_1`, and analog 0 (pad 63) is separate too. Will the Run 3 half-slot carrier keep that arrangement?
3. Leo, I noticed you added eFuses to one of your 3.3 V designs and then took them out about two weeks later. Was that for a supply, DRC/precheck or area reason? Anything I should watch out for?

I've also emailed info@wafer.space with the board-voltage part of this, and asked Egor about 3.3 V operation on the compiler's GitHub (egorxe/gf180_efuse_compiler#1). Posting here too in case the answers help others. Thanks!

[2026-09-27 8:22 a.m.] mole99
Hi @heathcliff,

1. Yes, the precheck should pass. The `Pad` layer (pad opening) is checked against a golden reference (first AND and then XOR) to ensure that the bonding machine has something to bond to. Which circuit is connected to the bond pad is entirely up to you.

2. We didn't plan on any functional changes to the CoB, but Andrew can confirm that for sure. As Mike mentioned, only the grounds are shorted together.

3. Yes, the reason is as Egor mentioned: 3.3V is not enough to blow the fuses, so I dropped the eFuses for this FPGA version, as I wanted to target 3.3V-only. In a future version, I would also like to have multiple power domains in the core and add the eFuses back.
