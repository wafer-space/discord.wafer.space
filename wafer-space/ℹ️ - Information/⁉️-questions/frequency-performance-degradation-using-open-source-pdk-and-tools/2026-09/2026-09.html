namibj 2026-09-17 10:40 a.m.
You'll have to use the Avalon Semiconductors 3v3 SCL to have a good chance; there's an SRAM compiler giving much better density than the foundry open PDK macros offer by @Chips4Makers aka Staf Verhaegen . Note that the Avalon 3v3 SCL isn't currently too production-ready but that's mostly because there may be bugs left that haven't yet been caught through extracted SPICE behavioral sanity checking. For purposes of determining if you can do an RTL on a wafer.space slot at least near a target speed, it should suffice.

There are some AI skills in HighTide for tips to improve PPA: https://github.com/VLSIDA/HighTide/blob/main/.claude%2Fskills%2Foptimize-ppa%2FSKILL.md As an aside though, clock skew is often the culprit. OR packs macros into one clock tree so if you have a bunch of SRAMs, for example, you could have a lot of skew between those and your logic. Check out how much skew affects your critical path... Similar to that, the default 20% of the period for input and output delays may not make sense in many cases.
