[2026-09-28 8:08 a.m.] 246tnt
* GND pads must be identical to the defaults ( They are all shorted together and to the chip backside )


[2026-09-28 8:09 a.m.] 246tnt
* Power pads are routed independently but they have decoupling caps, so you can pretty much only use them for power, or bias signals.


[2026-09-28 8:09 a.m.] 246tnt
* Signal pads can be anything, doesn't matter if you use them as input/output/bidir or analog.
