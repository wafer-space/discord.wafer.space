[2026-10-13 11:31 a.m.] mole99
Hi! You tell LibreLane what clock period should be achieved using the `CLOCK_PERIOD` variable.
If that clock period cannot be achieved for your design, the flow will error out.

Once you have your chip, you can supply an external clock signal up to that frequency. Of course, you can always supply a lower clock frequency (if you don't use dynamic flip-flops, which is the case for the default SCL).

[2026-10-14 6:27 a.m.] mole99
Hold time is independent of the clock period/frequency. Clock tree synthesis and the resizer ensure that your design is free from hold violations over all specified corners, and OpenSTA will verify this after parasitic extraction.
