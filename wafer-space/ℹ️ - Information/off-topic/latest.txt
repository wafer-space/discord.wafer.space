I then asked  "do you know *any* wirebonders?" 
And he said "Yeah! Literally downstairs, you want me to call him?"
I obviously said yes lol. 
The guy didn't really have a lot of useful information that was sharable. He sent me to this [product](https://www.yamaha-robotics.com/en/products/process/utc-rz1) and while it does have specs it wasn't what I was hoping for.

He drew some stuff on the whiteboard for me and then erased it before I could take a pic.
